is measured, not estimated — no third-party volume data was used, and the
reason is in the last section.

Position 6.4 is the surprise. When this site appears, it appears near the top
of page 1. The problem is not ranking. It is that almost nothing is eligible to
rank, and that one page is absorbing everything that is.

**The homepage takes 547 of 702 impressions — 78%.** That is what Google does
when it cannot find a more specific page: it falls back to the one page it
trusts. Every impression the homepage earns for "gujarati mirror work kurtis"
is an impression a dedicated page should have earned, with a better title, a
better snippet and a product grid instead of a hero image.

## The honest answer on Semrush and Ahrefs

They would not help yet, and it is worth being precise about why.

Semrush and Ahrefs are good at one thing: estimating how much search volume
exists for keywords you do not currently rank for. That is the right tool when
you have an indexed site and need to decide what to build next.

This site has **104 pages Google knows about and has not indexed** — including
almost every landing page in the table above. Paying for keyword research now
means planning traffic for pages that cannot receive any. The keywords are not
the bottleneck. Eligibility is.

78 of those are "Discovered – currently not indexed", which means Google has
the URL and has chosen not to spend crawl budget on it. On a domain a few
months old with no backlinks, that is normal and it resolves with authority
and time, not with better keywords.

Come back to Ahrefs when the indexed count is past 80 and there is a ranking
baseline worth optimising against. Right now Search Console is both free and
more truthful.

## What to do, in order

1. **Request indexing** in Search Console for the pages in the table above,
   starting with `/shop/mirror-work-kurta-sets` and `/shop/cotton-co-ord-sets`.
   Manual, one URL at a time, and the fastest lever available.
2. **Put "Gujarati" in the mirror-work page titles and copy.** The winning
   query is "gujarati mirror work kurtis"; the page is titled "Mirror Work
   Kurta Sets". The word that is earning the impressions is missing from the
   page earning them.
3. **Rewrite the homepage title and description** so brand searchers click. 62
   impressions at position 4.9 with no clicks is a snippet failing to answer
   "is this the shop I am looking for".
4. **Leave the rest alone** until the indexed count moves. Nothing else on
   this list matters while 104 pages are invisible.
